Your face

Add several photos of the same person (different angles and lighting help). Their face is blended into one identity and swapped onto the main character of every generated card. The swap itself runs locally and free; character cards may be regenerated up to 3 times (extra Cloudflare generations) until the art has a large, front-facing face.
